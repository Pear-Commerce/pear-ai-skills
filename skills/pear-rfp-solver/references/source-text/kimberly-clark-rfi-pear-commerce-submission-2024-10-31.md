## Page 29

Mid-funnel: Consideration
Brands have ignored the mid-funnel
Due to lack of inventory and performance data, unreliable shoppable experiences, and expensive
conversion rates to send to retailer PDPs, brands have specialized spend on awareness and
purchase, ignoring the mid-funnel.
Nurture Engaged Consumers
Keep consumers and potential repeat customers engaged with deliberate retargeting efforts, and
build lookalike audiences to acquire more.
Encourage Trial
Shoppers trial at retail, so the mid-funnel is the ideal audience to target and retarget to build
habitual purchasing.
Run Mid-Funnel Awareness Campaigns
Certain awareness ads (e.g. sweepstakes, incentives, or limited time releases) would be more
effective if served to a brand’s mid-funnel audience.

## Page 30

How Can Pear Help Improve Awareness Efforts?
Redefining awareness
For many brands, awareness means less “does this product exist?” and more
“where is this product available for purchase today?” Sending to a shoppable store
locator, which produces the highest-intent unsegmented audience you’ll own, can
be the perfect media destination.
Audiences of Brand Engagers
Awareness campaigns can create large digital audiences of the shoppers who
engage with your brand, which can be perfect to retarget with promos, product
launches, or other campaigns and to build lookalikes for customer acquisition.
Brand.com Pages Becoming Media Destinations
Rather than sending to a shoppable landing page, brands are using shoppable,
owned properties - like their PDPs - for better website engagement without losing
shoppability, data, or audiences.
