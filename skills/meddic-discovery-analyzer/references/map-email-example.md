# MAP Email Example

The following is a well-formatted mutual action plan email. Use it as a reference for tone, structure, length, and MAP table format.

---

**Subject:** Following up — Acme Corp × Velosify next steps

Hi Sarah,

Really appreciated your candor today — especially your point about reps not trusting the data they're currently getting. That's exactly the kind of friction we designed Velosify to fix, and it gave us a clear target for the proposal we're putting together.

**What we covered:**
- Current state: Acme's field team has low confidence in the existing data platform; adoption is under 20% and managers are pulling targets manually
- Trial results: 4 reps in the Southwest region uncovered $1.1M in net-new pipeline in 30 days using Velosify's proximity targeting and referral mapping
- Pricing: We walked through a 500-seat enterprise structure at $1,100/seat annually, all-inclusive (onboarding, CSM, CRM integration)
- Internal path: You need buy-in from Derek Chen (CFO) and the RevOps team before moving to contract; budget cycle opens Q1

**What we're sending you by Friday:**
- A stair-step proposal with a phased deployment option (start with 100 seats, expand on success criteria)
- A one-page ROI summary you can share with Derek — built on the trial data, not our estimates
- A side-by-side data comparison vs. your current platform, focused on the specific codes your team works

**Proposed next steps:**

| Action | Owner | Target date |
|--------|-------|-------------|
| Send phased proposal + ROI one-pager | Velosify (Jamie) | Fri Apr 4 |
| Review proposal and share with Derek | Acme (Sarah) | Wed Apr 9 |
| Intro call with Derek to discuss ROI case | Both | Week of Apr 14 |
| Confirm RevOps team availability for data comparison | Acme (Sarah) | Fri Apr 11 |
| Follow-up check-in if no response by | Velosify (Jamie) | Mon Apr 14 |

Does this look right to you? Happy to adjust any of the dates or add anything I missed. If it works, just reply with a thumbs up and I'll get moving on the proposal.

Talk soon,
Jamie

---

## What Makes This Email Work

- **Opening is specific** — references something real Sarah said, not generic praise
- **"What we covered" is buyer-centric** — frames the discussion around Acme's challenges, not Velosify's features
- **Seller commits first** — "what we're sending" leads before asking anything of the buyer
- **MAP table is clean** — 5 rows, named owners, specific dates, no jargon
- **CTA is frictionless** — "just reply with a thumbs up" makes it easy to confirm without a meeting

## What to Avoid

❌ "It was great speaking with you today and learning more about your business" — generic, adds nothing
❌ Listing every topic discussed — keep "what we covered" to the 3–5 things that actually matter
❌ Vague MAP entries like "Follow up on proposal" with no date or owner
❌ Internal language: "next step is to get to the economic buyer" — never in a buyer-facing email
❌ Emails over 400 words — if the buyer has to scroll, you've lost them
